# Session Summary: 2025-12-02

## What We Built

### 1. Real Piper TTS Integration
- ARPABET → IPA phoneme mapping for direct synthesis
- `phoneme_ids_to_audio()` for high-fidelity phoneme generation
- Training results: **99.4% accuracy** with CNN encoder (up from 63% ceiling)

### 2. CNN Encoder (Breaking the 63% Ceiling)
- New `CNNMelEncoder` class with 4-layer 2D convolutions
- Captures formants, temporal transitions, phoneme boundaries
- Results: 99.4% vs 73% linear encoder in 20 epochs

### 3. Phase 2: Speech Production Loop
- **6D Latent Phoneme Space** with articulatory anchors
  - Dimensions: front-back, high-low, rounded, voiced, manner, vowel/consonant
  - 41 phonemes positioned by linguistic features
- **Dual-Head Architecture**: perception + production sharing encoder
- **Self-Listening Loop**: agent speaks → hears itself → learns
- **Babbling→Imitation Curriculum**: 80% explore → 20% over training

### Training Results

**Progressive Curriculum (Final Run):**
| Phase | Task | Epochs | Match Rate |
|-------|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | 5 vowels | 50 | **100%** (5/5) |
| 2 | +5 consonants | 50 | **100%** (10/10) |
| 3 | All 40 phonemes | 100 | **100%** (40/40) |
| 4 | 13 word sequences | 100 | **100%** (13/13) |

**Words/Syllables Learned:**
- Simple: ba, bee, ma, me, hi, go, no
- Complex: yes, mom, dad, hello, food, water

Key achievements:
- Distinguished **voiced/unvoiced pairs** (B↔P, D↔T) - single dimension flip
- Recognizes first phoneme of full words (hello→HH, water→W)
- Progressive curriculum enables learning without catastrophic forgetting
