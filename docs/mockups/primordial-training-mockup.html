Training System

Understanding how your agents develop. Stats grow from behavior - "use it, build it."

Core Philosophy

"Stats are autobiography, not biography."

Your agent's stats tell the story of what it survived. You don't assign stats with sliders - your agent earns them through experience. An agent that runs a lot becomes fast. An agent that escapes predators develops quick reflexes. The stats reflect the life lived.

Behavior → Stat Mapping
🏃 Max Speed
Trigger: Sustained running at 50%+ max speed for ~1 second
⚡ Reaction Time
Trigger: Escaping from predator danger zone without taking damage
👁 Vision Range
Trigger: Moving toward food spotted at edge of vision range
👂 Hearing Range
Trigger: Reacting to sounds before visual confirmation
🟢 Efficiency
Trigger: Surviving and moving while below 30% energy
💪 Max Health
Trigger: Taking damage, surviving, and recovering
Permanent vs Active Gains

When your agent trains a stat, the gain is split into two parts:

Base (150)
Perm (+12)
Active (+30)
Example: Max Speed = 150 + 12 + 30 = 192 Total trained: +42 (12 permanent, 30 active)

Permanent Gains (20%)

  • Never decay
  • Represent "muscle memory"
  • Transfer to clones
  • Your baseline improvement

Active Gains (80%)

  • Decay slowly when not exercised
  • Represent "current fitness"
  • Transfer to clones at current level
  • Keeps specialists specialized

Why decay?

Decay ensures your agents stay specialized. Without it, every agent would eventually max out all stats. With decay, a "Sprinter" who stops running will gradually become less fast, making room for other abilities. This creates meaningful choices about how to train your agents.
